Purpose of the Portal

The Student Information Portal is designed to provide easy access to important university information, resources, and services. It serves as a central point for students to stay informed about their academic journey and university life.

Features of the Portal

  1. Student's registration
  2. Access to course materials and resources
  3. View academic calendar and important dates
  4. Check grades and academic progress
  5. Receive announcements and updates from the university
  6. Connect with faculty and staff for support
